Our learning approach

About FE Academy

FE Academy was established to make programming, technology and practical learning easier to understand through guided practice, patient explanations and practical projects. It was founded by Firdaus and Khairi, and later expanded into business and data learning with Asyraf.

Classes are open to learners of all ages and backgrounds. Most monthly-plan classes are conducted in groups. When only one learner joins a scheduled class, the class can continue as an individual session at the same monthly plan price, subject to trainer availability.

Why “Eisner”? It is a reference to Byleth Eisner, the Fire Emblem hero who is also a teacher — a small nod to guidance, growth and learning together. ^_^

Structured three-month programmes

Programme Syllabuses

Each structured programme runs for an average of 12 weeks. Monthly assessments help learners review their progress. Technology programmes normally finish with a practical project, while English and Maths programmes include level-appropriate communication or problem-solving tasks and a final assessment.

Programmes can be adjusted to suit the learner's age, experience, starting level and learning speed. English and Maths learners can be placed at Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced level based on current ability rather than age alone. Some technology programmes require suitable software or hardware.
